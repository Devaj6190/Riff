# Riff — glossary

- **Riff**: the product, and also one two-player game session that players create and share by link or 4-letter code (`riffs` table, `/r/<code>`). Never "room".
- **Player**: one of the two people in a riff, sitting in **seat** `A` (creator) or `B` (joiner).
- **Round**: one timed prompt both players answer, of one **mechanic** (open prompt, two truths, image, pick, voice, meme audio).
- **Template**: a JSON seed the AI rewrites into a round for this pair.
- **Talk window**: the free-chat gap after each round's result.
- **Bonus Round**: a catch-up round on the trailing player's turf, worth 2×.
- **Depth**: how personal a round is, 1 (light) to 3 (deep). Rises over the riff.
- **Connection points**: judge points for follow-ups and callbacks, in rounds and talk windows.
